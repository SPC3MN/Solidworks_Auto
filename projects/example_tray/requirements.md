# Example tray: requirements

Stand-in for a photo brief, used to dry-run the photo-to-model workflow.

- Open-top tray, outside 80 × 50 × 25 mm, vertical corner radius 6 mm.
- Wall and floor thickness 2 mm (shell, open at the top).
- Four Ø3.2 mm through holes in the floor (M3 clearance) at (±30, ±17) mm
  from the centre.
- Material: PLA, FDM.

Expected volume, worked out by hand:
outer area 80·50 − (4−π)·6² = 3969.097 mm²; inner area 76·46 − (4−π)·4² = 3482.266 mm²;
3969.097·25 − 3482.266·23 = 19135.32 mm³; minus the holes 4·π·1.6²·2 = 64.34 mm³
→ **19070.98 mm³**.
