# USB-C power cable

Plugs into the Pi 5 USB-C socket. The socket is modelled solid, so the overmould butts against its face and the tongue is left out. Sizes **estimated**:

- Overmould 11 × 6 × 20; strain relief Ø4.5 × 8.
- Lead Ø3.5: 5 mm straight, then a 30 mm-radius bend that turns it toward −x, then 40 mm straight. It stays at the socket's height, so in the assembly it sits 1.45 mm above the table.
