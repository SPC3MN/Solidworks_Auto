# RCA audio cable (plug + lead)

Plugs into the DAC+ HAT's RCA sockets (barrel Ø8, sticks out 9 mm, bore Ø5). All sizes **estimated** for a typical moulded lead:

- Sleeve: OD 10, ID 8 (a sliding fit over the barrel), 7 mm long. Centre pin Ø1.5, 7 mm long, into the bore.
- Handle Ø11 × 20; strain relief Ø7 × 10.
- Lead Ø5, swept along a path that lies naturally:
  - 5 mm straight;
  - a 20 mm-radius bend downward;
  - straight down;
  - a 15 mm-radius bend onto the table, which is 70.2 mm below the socket axis (the axis height in the exploded assembly);
  - 40 mm along the table.
