# Example box: requirements

The smallest design that exercises the whole pipeline.

- Solid rectangular block, 100 × 60 × 30 mm (L × W × H).
- Material: PLA, FDM printed.
- Exports: native part, STEP, STL.

Assumptions: no holes, fillets or shell yet. Those come in later examples
(tests 3 to 5 in docs/architecture.md).
