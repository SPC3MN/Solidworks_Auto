# airmn_impeller_housing

Lid round the impeller: solid 430 wall seated in the inlet ring's rabbet, 8 mm top plate perforated with 7.2 holes on a 8.57 hex pitch (64 % open) for a vertical outlet; tie rods end in nut counterbores in the top.

- Material: PETG
- Expected volume 1072098.0 mm3, bbox 430.0 x 430.0 x 173.0 mm (from `airmn_parts.py`).
- All sizes: `parameters/params.json`; sources and assumptions: [../airmn/requirements.md](../airmn/requirements.md).
